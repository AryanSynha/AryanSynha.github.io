BIOGRAPHY / THE LORE

About me

[ A-01 ]

I've been interested in computers and technology since I was a kid. The curiosity never really went away; it just got more specific. These days, I’m drawn to the systems underneath the interface: terminals, networks, operating systems, and the details most people never need to think about.

Cybersecurity is the rabbit hole I'm most interested in exploring. I want to understand how software and systems fail, how they can be defended, and what it takes to go from following a tutorial to genuinely knowing what I'm doing. I'm not claiming expert status — this site is a record of the learning process.
